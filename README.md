# Awesome-Time-Card-Management

## Top Time Card Management Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Timesheet Tracking, Project Billing & Workforce Time Intelligence*  

**Last updated: October 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Time Card Management**. These tools enable organizations to record employee and contractor hours, manage project-based time allocation, generate invoices from billable time, and analyze workforce productivity across teams and engagements.



**Examples** include ServiceNow Time Card Management, Harvest, Toggl Track, Replicon, Clockify, ClickTime, Time Doctor, Everhour, Hubstaff, and QuickBooks Time (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with active projects for self-hosted timesheet tracking, project time billing, and workforce attendance — ideal for freelancers, agencies, and enterprises building sovereign time management infrastructure without per-seat pricing lock-in.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[ServiceNow Time Card Management](https://www.servicenow.com/)**  

  Enterprise time card and timesheet management integrated with Project Portfolio Management and GRC Audit Management; supports time logging against engagements, tasks, issues, and milestones with approval routing based on timesheet policies .



- **[Harvest](https://www.getharvest.com/)**  

  Time tracking and invoicing platform for teams and freelancers, with project budgets, billable rates, and invoice generation from tracked hours.



- **[Toggl Track](https://toggl.com/track/)**  

  Simple, cross-platform time tracking tool with one-click timers, project tagging, and reporting for individuals and teams.



- **[Replicon](https://www.replicon.com/)**  

  Enterprise time and attendance platform with timesheet approval workflows, payroll integration, and project time tracking.



- **[Clockify](https://clockify.me/)**  

  Free time tracker with unlimited users, project tracking, timesheets, and reporting; widely adopted for its generous free tier.



- **[ClickTime](https://www.clicktime.com/)**  

  Time and expense tracking platform with project budgeting, resource planning, and timesheet approvals for professional services teams.



- **[Time Doctor](https://www.timedoctor.com/)**  

  Workforce productivity and time tracking with screenshots, app/URL monitoring, and payroll integration for remote teams.



- **[Everhour](https://everhour.com/)**  

  Time tracking and budgeting tool that integrates with project management platforms like Asana, Trello, and Jira.



- **[Hubstaff](https://hubstaff.com/)**  

  Time tracking and workforce management with screenshots, activity monitoring, geofencing, and payroll for distributed teams.



- **[QuickBooks Time](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/time-tracking/)**  

  Time tracking (formerly TSheets) integrated with QuickBooks for payroll, job costing, and employee scheduling.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Kimai](https://github.com/kimai/kimai)**  

  The leading open-source time-tracking application (AGPL-3.0, ~4,500 GitHub stars); web-based multi-user platform for timesheets, project/customer/activity management, invoicing (PDF/DOCX/HTML/XLSX/ODS), reporting, and LDAP/SAML authentication; available as self-hosted or Kimai Cloud SaaS with GDPR-compliant EU hosting .



- **[Odoo Timesheets](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  

  Open-source ERP with a comprehensive timesheet module; features timer start/stop, project/task encoding, overtime visibility, allocated hours tracking, team validation workflows, invoicing from timesheets, and grid/kanban/pivot/graph views .



- **[OrangeHRM](https://github.com/orangehrm/orangehrm)**  

  Open-source HR management system with a full attendance and time module; supports punch in/out, attendance sheets, pay policies, selfie/geolocation capture, and automated early departure/excess duration tracking in recent releases .



- **[Titra](https://github.com/kromitgmbh/titra)**  

  Modern open-source project time tracking for freelancers and small teams; built with Meteor and React, focused on simplicity and clean time entry workflows .



- **[TimeTagger](https://github.com/almarklein/timetagger)**  

  Open-source time tracker based on an interactive timeline with powerful reporting; supports tagging, filtering, and a visual approach to reviewing where time went .



- **[ActivityWatch](https://github.com/ActivityWatch/activitywatch)**  

  Open-source automated time tracker that runs locally and tracks application/window usage; useful for understanding actual time allocation without manual timers .



- **[Worklenz](https://github.com/Worklenz/worklenz)**  

  All-in-one open-source project management tool that includes time tracking alongside task management, suitable for teams needing integrated project-time workflows .



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Miru** — Time tracking, invoicing, and employee benefits built with Ruby on Rails and ReactJS .

- **klog** — Command-line time tracking in a human-readable plain-text file format, ideal for developers who prefer file-based workflows .

- **solidtime** — Open-source time tracker with desktop apps for macOS, Windows, and Linux .

- **Zeiterfassung (urlaubsverwaltung)** — Digital working time capture and verification for German workplace compliance requirements .



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Kimai** for full time-tracking and invoicing, **Odoo Timesheets** for ERP-integrated time management, **OrangeHRM** for attendance and HR compliance, and **ActivityWatch** for passive time intelligence in a fully self-hosted time card management stack.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Time card management platforms handling employee data must comply with applicable labor laws, payroll regulations, and data privacy requirements (GDPR, CCPA, etc.).

- Self-hosted open-source solutions require proper security hardening, backup planning, and integration with payroll/HR systems.

- Verify license terms for commercial use; Kimai uses AGPL-3.0, Odoo uses LGPL/AGPL variants, and OrangeHRM uses GPL-2.0.
